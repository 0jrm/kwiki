**Field definitions:**
- `confidence`: float 0.0–1.0. Estimate of claim reliability. High (≥0.8) = multiple primary sources + cross-verified. Medium (0.4–0.79) = single source or inferred. Low (<0.4) = ambiguous, speculative, or unverified. Default: `0.5`
- `sources_count`: integer. Count of distinct sources that contributed knowledge to this page. Default: `1`
- `last_confirmed`: ISO 8601 datetime. When was this content last reviewed and still deemed accurate? Set to ingest time on creation. Default: ingest timestamp.
- `decay_rate`: string enum. How quickly does this content go stale? `"low"` = timeless concepts and mental models. `"medium"` = tools, libraries, team practices. `"high"` = versions, release notes, current events, pricing. Default: `"medium"`
